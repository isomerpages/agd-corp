<h1>Internship Opportunities</h1>
<p>We offer internship programmes to pre-university and undergraduate students
who wish to gain experience in accounting and finance related work in the
Public Sector.</p>
<div class="iframe-wrapper">
<iframe referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" height="315" width="560" allowfullscreen="true" frameborder="0" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/6e56NB6t3jE?si=1g9S_H-g0XK0ZQbJ&amp;vq=hd1080p"></iframe>
</div>
<p>Under this programme, interns will get the opportunity to work in a wide
variety of challenging projects, gaining valuable and practical work experience.
The internship experience will also help them develop an interdisciplinary
perspective, giving them a significant edge when seeking future employment.</p>
<p><strong>Under the internship programme, opportunities include:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Participating in comprehensive assessments of government operations and
expenditure effectiveness, including field visits and stakeholder meetings
to gather direct insights.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Contributing to research projects examining global developments and emerging
trends that inform policy direction and operational improvements.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>WWork alongside our officers on key initiatives including Data Analytics
and benchmarking of financial processes and functions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Deliver project outcomes and recommendations to management and senior
leadership teams, translating insights into actionable strategies.
<br>
</p>
